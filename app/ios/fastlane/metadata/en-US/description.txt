Browse and request movies, shows, books, and music. Follow downloads and get notified when something is ready. Manage your media services and investigate stuck downloads from your phone or tablet.

Cantinarr connects to a server you run on your computer or NAS. The server is open source and runs in one Docker container. Set it up at cantinarr.com, then connect the app with a link from your administrator.

FOR YOUR HOUSEHOLD

• Explore movies and shows from TMDB and Trakt, plus books and music from your connected services
• Search across your catalogs and see whether a title is Available, Requested, or Downloading
• Request a movie, show, selected seasons, ebook, audiobook, or album
• Follow request and download progress, with push notifications when content is ready
• Report a problem with a title so an administrator can investigate it

Your administrator chooses which services and titles you can access, and which requests need approval. Connect links make sign-in simple; passwords and passkeys are available when enabled.

FOR ADMINS

• Review requests and set approval, quality, and access rules for your household
• Manage Radarr, Sonarr, Chaptarr, Lidarr, and supported download clients from one app
• Open a series, season, or episode, search for releases, and check live queues
• Use Import Doctor to understand stuck downloads and review proposed fixes
• Share Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, and Audiobookshelf access; check activity through Tautulli or Tracearr
• Set up push notifications, accounts, services, and devices from the app

An AI provider is optional. If you connect one, the assistant can look up your library, suggest titles, and make requests. The repair agent can investigate persistent download problems and bring actions to an administrator for review. Cantinarr also offers MCP tools for clients you authorize.

PRIVACY

The app connects to your own server. Service credentials stay server-side. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking. Poster art loads from the TMDB image CDN. AI features send prompts and context to the provider you or your administrator choose. If you link ChatGPT, sign-in happens in your browser; your password never passes through Cantinarr.
