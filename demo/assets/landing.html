Sign in as admin to manage instances, users, access, queues, monitoring, issues, and proposed repairs.

Create or link simulated Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, and Audiobookshelf access, then choose how Watch and Listen links open.

Explore freely. All content is public domain or fictional. Every integration is simulated, and nothing here touches a real media server.
